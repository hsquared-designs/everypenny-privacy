Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This Privacy Notice for H-Squared Designs Ltd. ('we', 'us', or 'our'), the maker of EveryPenny, describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ('process') your personal information when you use our services ('Services'), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application (EveryPenny: Personal Finance Manager), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Use EveryPenny. EveryPenny is an iPhone app for managing personal finances. People add or import their spending (from Excel, Numbers, CSV or bank files), enter their payslip, and track their budget, bills and subscriptions, savings goals, debts and optional tithe and offering. The app works in British pounds, US dollars, euros or Jamaican dollars. Money data is stored on the person’s iPhone and protected with Face ID. EveryPenny doesn’t connect to bank accounts. People create an account with their email (signing in with a one-time code), Apple or Google. Optional AI insights send only summary totals, never individual transactions, to EveryPenny’s server, which uses Anthropic’s Claude to write personalised tips; people can switch AI off at any time. EveryPenny is a subscription app with a 30-day free trial, then a monthly or yearly plan paid through the App Store. Accounts can be deleted at any time from within the app.
  • Contact us for support or with questions about your privacy

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at everypennyprivacy@gmail.com.

12. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ('DNT') feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognising or honouring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.

14. HOW EVERYPENNY STORES YOUR MONEY DATA

Your transactions, payslip, bills, goals, debts and giving settings are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers. They are protected by your device’s security and, if you turn it on, Face ID or your passcode. Face ID is handled entirely by your iPhone. We never receive your face or fingerprint data. 15. AI INSIGHTS

If you use AI insights or “Ask about your money”, we send summary totals (such as spending by category, bill and goal amounts, payslip figures and, if you’ve turned it on, tithe and offering totals) and your questions to our service provider Anthropic, which generates the response.
