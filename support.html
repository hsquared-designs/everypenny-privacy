Common questions

How does the free trial work?

You get 30 days free from the day you create your account, with no payment details needed. After that, you can choose EveryPenny Premium: £2.99 a month or £19.99 a year (UK prices; prices in other countries are shown in the app).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap EveryPenny, then Cancel Subscription.

Or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I've already subscribed. How do I get Premium back?

In EveryPenny, go to Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the EveryPenny purchase.

Where is my money data kept?

Your money data is stored on your iPhone. EveryPenny doesn't connect to your bank. AI insights are off unless you turn them on, and even then only summary totals are used, never individual transactions. Read the Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I move to a new iPhone?
  1. On your old iPhone, go to Settings → Your data → Back up to Files and save the backup to iCloud Drive.
  2. On your new iPhone, install EveryPenny and sign in.
  3. Go to Settings → Your data → Restore from a backup and choose the file.
Which files can I import and export?

You can import spending from Excel, Numbers, CSV, OFX and QIF files, and export to Excel, Numbers, CSV or PDF. Go to Settings → Import & export.

I've forgotten my PIN

On the lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? Use your iPhone passcode. Once you're in, you can set a new PIN in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In EveryPenny, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the money data on your iPhone, so you may want to back up first. Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription; cancel it in your iPhone's Settings as shown above.
